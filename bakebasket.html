What It Does

// 01

Recipe Upload

Add recipes with a full set of details: ingredients with quantities, step-by-step instructions, custom tags, and photos. The ingredient checklist makes it easy to follow along while baking without losing your place.

Custom Tags Photo Upload Ingredient Checklist Instructions
// 02

Share with Friends

Send any recipe directly to another user in the app. Perfect for a baker who wants to share her recipes with followers or collaborators. No copying links, no screenshotting posts.

User Sharing In-App
// 03

Sourdough Calculator

A built-in hydration and ratio calculator tailored to sourdough baking. Scale recipes up or down and automatically adjust ingredient quantities based on your desired batch size or hydration level.

Hydration Calculator Batch Scaling Auto Adjust
// 04

Shopping Lists

Generate a shopping list from any recipe in one tap. Ingredients pull directly from the recipe so you never have to re-enter anything. Lists are saved in the app until you clear them.

Auto-Generate Saved Lists
